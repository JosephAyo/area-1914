# Living Archive design references

Reviewed through web text on 10 November 2026. These are now explicit research inputs; the original proposal was conceptual. Layout and typography observations require rendered inspection before visual approval. References guide our own composition and do not grant permission to reuse assets.

## The Republic

Source: [The Republic](https://rpublc.com/).

The publication organizes stories into a lead, weekly selections, regions, and topics, with introductions that help readers choose. Apply that editorial hierarchy to one homepage lead and secondary browsing routes. Avoid copying its branding or presenting this app as a news publication with an editorial reporting team. Its content structure supports the proposal; our exact type and color choices remain hypotheses.

## The Centenary Project

Source: [Pan-Atlantic University's Centenary Project on Google Arts and Culture](https://artsandculture.google.com/partner/pan-atlantic-university).

The collection presents Nigerian cultural material through objects and themed stories, including textiles, food, and art. Apply the combination of collection browsing and contextual journeys. Keep communities and practices specific; do not use one decorative motif as shorthand for all Nigerian history. Referencing the collection does not authorize copying its imagery.

## Datawrapper

Source: [What to consider when using text in data visualizations](https://www.datawrapper.de/blog/text-in-data-visualizations).

Apply nearby labels, explicit units, clear wording, and restrained text hierarchy to the attention chart. The chart's supporting text is part of the design. Use sourced annotations only when they support the intended claim; pageview peaks alone do not establish causes.

## Two study hypotheses

| Study               | Composition                                                                                                     | Intended strength                                                | Question to resolve                                                      |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| A Editorial edition | Paper background, serif display type, large headline beside a modest archive portrait, compact attention column | Clear reading hierarchy and room for substantial subject context | Does it feel distinctive with ordinary or absent imagery?                |
| B Collection plate  | Dark ink lead, oversized sans title, portrait held as a catalog object, numbered record details                 | Stronger collection identity and an image-led entrance           | Does the display treatment stay readable and leave enough room for data? |

Both retain the current product name and use the same lead subject, narrative, connection examples, and illustrative chart. Switching styles must not change the dataset and distort the comparison.
